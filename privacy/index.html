Privacy policy: The Chozeh

The Chozeh is a private dashboard used only by the owner of the accounts it reads, Rav Shlomo Katz: rabbi of Kehillat Shirat David in Efrat, Torah teacher and musician in the Carlebach tradition; shiurim, Chassidus, niggunim and concerts, for English- and Hebrew-speaking audiences. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the owner and the people they share the password with.

  • It reads only the owner's own accounts, through each platform's official API and with the owner's permission: numbers about the owner's posts, followers and audience, and the text of comments on the owner's posts. It does not read or store private messages.
  • Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, reads new posts and comments to tag the posts and pick out comments worth the owner's attention. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Commenters' names and handles are not stored. Comment text is kept for at most 30 days.
  • The data is stored in the owner's private GitHub repository and shown only on a password-protected page. It is never sold or shared with anyone else.
  • A comment deleted on its platform is dropped on the next update.
  • To have anything removed, contact the account owner through the account itself.
